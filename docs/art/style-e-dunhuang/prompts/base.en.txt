Style-e / Style Five: Dunhuang mineral flat painting, bestiary cutouts, not a cave-mural story illustration.

Simplified decorative mural language: flat mineral fills, clean dark outlines, large color blocks, paper-cutout silhouettes. Graphic game concept art, readable at small size. Not ultra-detailed fresco, not oil painting, not realistic lighting, not 3D shading, not impasto, not cinematic volume light.

Solid flat background only (azurite / mineral stone-blue shiqing preferred; pale mural-cream allowed). No environment, no landscape, no floor plane, no ground perspective, no clouds-as-scene, no temple, no palace hall, no cave wall as setting.

Each subject is an isolated cutout with generous empty background around it. No overlapping, no intertwining ribbons between figures, no foreground-background fusion.

Low ornament. Few particles. No inscription walls. No flying apsara crowds.

Honghuang / Fengshen xianxia costume language for production (Daoist robes, hunter armor, fox-court dress) unless a card is explicitly Buddhist.
