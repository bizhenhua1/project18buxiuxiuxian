Artifact subject ({NAME}):

A standalone magic tool icon, isolated cutout, not held unless the shot is a character card. Whole object readable as a toy-like mineral-gold and stone-green graphic. No shrine, no altar, no hands.

Draw as a prop on empty flat background. No Sanskrit, no Buddhist dharma seal, no prayer beads, no alms bowl unless the card is explicitly that relic.

Material: matte mineral gold, malachite inlay, cinnabar accents. Flat, not chrome, not jewel-shader.

Examples to substitute:
- Three-pointed double-edged spear (san jian dao): polearm silhouette, not a crucifix, not a khakkhara.
- Golden Flood-Dragon Scissors (Jinjiao Jian): two linked dragon-head shears.
- Heaven-Overturning Seal (Fantian Yin): cube/stamp, simple cloud or mountain mark, not a sutra seal.
- Apricot-yellow banner (Xinghuang Qi): yellow flag, simple cloud emblem, not a Buddhist banner.
- Pipa lute or plain silk sash: gold and tan; sash is a folded/floating strip, not wrapped on a body.
- Wind-Fire Wheels: two flaming wheels, gold and cinnabar, isolated pair.
- Qiankun hoop: simple metal ring/hoop as object.
- God-Beating Whip (Dashen Bian): decorative whip or short flail-rod, NOT a spiked monk staff, NOT a tin khakkhara with rings.
- Huntian silk: a separate ribbon coil or stacked silk bundle, not worn, not apsara streamers around a person.
