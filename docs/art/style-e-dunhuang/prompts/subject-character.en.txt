Character subject ({NAME}, {ROLE}):

Fengshen / Daoist xianxia person, not a Buddhist saint unless specified. Clear full-body or 3/4-body paper-cutout silhouette. Simplified costume in mineral azurite, malachite, cinnabar, muted tan, matte gold. Hair in warrior topknot, double buns, fox-enchantress long hair, or Daoist pin — not a shaved monk head.

Stance: standing, readable weapon-or-empty hands. Not a hovering apsara, not a worshipper offering pose.

Costume: daopao, hunter outfit, simplified silver armor, court-immortal dress, or short battle clothes. NOT kasaya, NOT orange pilgrim robes.

Props in hand only if this card is the wielder. Long silk, Huntian sash, and similar must NOT wrap the body; those are separate artifact cards.

Gold disc behind the head: off by default. If on, a small simple circle smaller than the head, no rays.

Examples to substitute:
- Yang Jian: third vertical eye, hunter or silver armor, may hold three-pointed spear.
- Nezha: youthful warrior child, red-and-gold fighter clothes, not lotus child-saint; spear optional; silk and hoop not worn.
- Su Daji: fox-spirit woman, optional small fox ears, cinnabar court dress, not a nun.
- Jiejiao cultivator: daopao, cloud-collar, immortal topknot or wild hair, slightly fiercer, not serene bodhisattva.
- Jiang Ziya: elderly sage, long beard, tan and stone-green daopao; whip or short rod in hand, NOT a monk staff.
