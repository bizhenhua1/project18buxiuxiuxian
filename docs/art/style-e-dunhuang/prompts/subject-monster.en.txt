Clear species read: fox, dog, dragon-serpent, ape, tiger-hybrid, chimera, two-headed snake, taotie mask. Stone-green, gold, cinnabar, white-mineral as needed.

Not a Buddhist lion, not a white elephant mount of a bodhisattva, not Garuda, not a yaksha dharmapala, not a naga throne.
